# Mission Reflection
After completing this mission, I gained a deeper understanding of AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and their roles in real-world businesses. Before this activity, I knew that cloud platforms were mainly used to store data and run applications, but I did not completely understand the differences among the major cloud providers. Through my research, I discovered that while these platforms offer many similar services, each one has its own unique features and advantages.

Among the three cloud providers, AWS impressed me the most because of the wide variety of services it offers. Tools such as Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, Amazon RDS, and Amazon VPC provide solutions for computing, storage, databases, and networking. I also learned that AWS is suitable for both small startups and large organizations because its services can easily scale based on business needs.

Although AWS, Azure, and GCP have their differences, they also share many common features. All three offer virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and Kubernetes services. They allow businesses to access computing resources without needing to manage all of their own physical servers and hardware. In addition, each platform provides important features such as scalability, security, and high availability.

One of the most important lessons I learned is that business needs play a major role in choosing the right cloud platform. For instance, a university that already relies on Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may find Azure more suitable because of its strong integration with Microsoft products. Meanwhile, an AI research company may prefer GCP because of its advanced AI and machine learning services. On the other hand, a startup may select AWS because of its broad range of services and flexible scalability.

Through my role as a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I realized that no single cloud provider is the best choice for every organization. The right recommendation should depend on factors such as the company's budget, current technology, security requirements, performance needs, scalability, and future plans. This mission taught me that technology decisions should always focus on solving the specific needs and challenges of an organization.

Completing this mission also helped improve my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio. I was able to include research documents, comparison tables, client recommendations, screenshots, and my reflection. In addition, I gained more experience using Markdown, GitHub, and proper documentation techniques. Overall, this mission strengthened my knowledge of cloud computing and improved my research, analytical, documentation, and decision-making skills, which will be valuable in my future career as an IT professional.
